## Description

Draw Cartoons 2 is an animation editor for making your own movies and cartoons. Pose a character, set the next pose, and the app fills in the frames between them.

**Skeletal animation**
Move the bones. You do not redraw the character on every frame.

**Motion tweening**
Set the key poses. The app draws the frames in between.

**Item constructor**
Build your own characters and props, then drop them into a scene.

**Camera and speed**
Move and zoom the camera. Slow a scene down or speed it up.

**Library and backgrounds**
Use characters, weapons, props, and effects, or paint a background.

**Export**
Save the finished movie to Photos.

No account. The app runs in landscape.
